# The Faithful Friend — Production Package

16 November 2026

The brief named no subject, so this package proposes one: **bacalhau**, the salt cod at the centre of Portuguese cooking, made from a fish that does not live in Portuguese waters. The film is set in Lisbon and in Ílhavo and Gafanha da Nazaré, the old cod-fleet towns on the Aveiro lagoon.

This is the pre-production package (deliverables A–H). No footage has been filmed, licensed or AI-generated. A timed animatic built from this package is in [`deliverables/`](../deliverables); see [animatic-notes.md](animatic-notes.md) for what it is and is not. Every factual claim in the narration is traced to a source in section B; anything that depends on what the crew finds on location is marked as such.

## A. Editorial concept

**Working title:** *The Faithful Friend* (after *o fiel amigo*, the Portuguese nickname for salt cod).

**Central story.** Portugal's most emblematic food is a fish its own sea has never held. The film follows one salted cod from a Lisbon grocer's counter to a family pot, and asks how a cold-water stranger became the taste of home.

**Main discovery.** Atlantic cod's range ends at the Bay of Biscay, north of Portugal. The country did not inherit this food from its coast; it went and fetched it, first by sail and dory off Newfoundland and Greenland, now by pallet from Norway and Iceland. What is Portuguese about bacalhau is the cure and the kitchen.

**The angle that makes it worth telling.** The film opens on live cod swimming in a museum tank in Ílhavo, fish that came from Ålesund in Norway. A national symbol that has to be imported even to be looked at.

**Intended audience.** Adult viewers of cultural and travel programming in Europe and beyond; curious, well-travelled, sceptical of tourism films. No prior knowledge of Portugal assumed.

**Emotional objective.** Quiet surprise, then warmth. The viewer should finish with the sense that identity can be something a people chooses and works at, and with an appetite.

**Tone guardrails.** No presenter, no recipe demonstration, no nostalgia for the dictatorship that promoted the cod fleets. The dory years were hard and are treated as such.

### Context for the crew (not in narration)

- **Scale.** Portugal ate nearly 55,000 tonnes of salt cod in 2023, about 6 kg a head, according to the cod industry association (AIB). The sector employs over 2,500 people.
- **The cure.** The EU-protected *Bacalhau de Cura Tradicional Portuguesa* is dry-salted for at least 30 days, matured at least 30 more, then dried to no more than 47% moisture, by wind and sun or in a tunnel at 18–21 °C. The applicant body, AIB, is based on Avenida dos Bacalhoeiros in Gafanha da Nazaré.
- **The place.** Ílhavo and neighbouring Gafanha da Nazaré sit on the Aveiro lagoon. The museum ship *Santo André*, a cod trawler launched in 1948, is moored there (lead: [Visit Portugal](https://www.visitportugal.com/en/node/138302), not opened).
- **Grades.** Shops sell by weight class, from *miúdo* (under 500 g) to *especial* (over 4 kg).

### Potential factual pitfalls

- **"Portugal eats 20% of the world's cod."** Widely repeated, including by AFP, but no primary figure was found. Not used.
- **"365 recipes" or "1,001 recipes."** A saying, not a count. Not used.
- **"Portugal no longer fishes cod."** False. The EU observatory records Portuguese cod catches as low, not zero. The narration says only that the dories are gone.
- **Christmas Eve cod is not universal.** Sources say "in some parts of Portugal". The narration says "in many homes".
- **White hulls.** One unopened source says hulls were painted white in the Second World War to mark neutrality. Plausible, unverified, not used.
- **One man per dory.** Commonly described, but not confirmed in any source opened here. The narration gives no number; confirm with the Ílhavo museum curator.
- **"Half the year."** Rests on one weak source. Confirm season length with the museum before the script is locked.
- **The aquarium.** Described as unique in Portugal in 2012, when its opening was postponed after storm damage. Confirm it is operating, still stocked with *Gadus morhua*, and open to filming.
- **Species at the counter.** Other salted fish are sold alongside true cod. Film only fish the shop confirms as *Gadus morhua*.
- **Romanticising the fleet.** The cod campaign was a project of an authoritarian state; a fishermen's strike in 1937 is documented in Portuguese scholarship (lead, not opened). Archive must not be scored or cut as heroic.
- **People.** No individual is named or quoted in this package. Contributors are cast on the recce and identified only with consent.

## E. AI video generation prompts

The film uses two AI-generated shots, both of open sea where no specific place or person is claimed. A third prompt is a contingency if archive cannot be licensed. No AI is used for people at work, food, Lisbon, Ílhavo or the aquarium.

**Continuity for all three.** Same sea, same weather: grey North Atlantic under fog, no sun, no horizon line sharper than a smudge. Palette of slate, pewter and dull green; no teal, no orange. Natural motion at real speed, 25 fps, 16:9, no lens flare, no text, no birds added for effect.

### AI-01 — Shot 4, North Atlantic swell (5 s)

> Documentary aerial footage of the open North Atlantic in early spring. **Subject:** long, heavy grey swells, empty of ships, land and wildlife. **Location and environment:** far offshore in a cold northern sea; low fog bank merging sea and sky. **Composition:** water fills the lower four-fifths of the frame; a soft band of fog at the top; no hard horizon. **Camera framing:** wide aerial, high oblique angle of about 40 degrees, as from a helicopter at 150 metres. **Camera movement:** slow, steady forward drift, no tilt, no orbit. **Lighting:** flat diffuse overcast, no direct sun, no specular glitter. **Time of day:** mid-morning. **Visual mood:** cold, vast, indifferent, quiet. **Documentary realism:** natural wave physics and foam, slight atmospheric haze, the look of a 35 mm-sensor cinema camera with a 35 mm lens, restrained contrast, fine natural grain; nothing stylised. **Aspect ratio:** 16:9, 3840 × 2160, 25 fps, 5 seconds.

*Accuracy check:* no icebergs, no whales, no tropical blue, no coastline. This shot stands for "northern waters" and is not captioned as a named place.

### AI-02 — Shot 20, the dory (4 s), captioned *Reconstruction*

> Illustrative historical reconstruction, mid-twentieth century, in the style of sober observational documentary. **Subject:** a single small flat-bottomed wooden dory, about five metres long, with one fisherman seated, seen from behind and at a distance; he wears dark oilskins and a sou'wester and is hauling a handline over the side. His face is not visible. **Location and environment:** open sea on a northern fishing bank; dense fog; no other vessel in sight. **Composition:** the dory small in the frame, left of centre, lower third; grey water and fog around it. **Camera framing:** wide shot from near water level, as from another small boat. **Camera movement:** camera rises and falls gently with the swell; no push-in, no orbit. **Lighting:** soft, shadowless fog light. **Time of day:** early morning. **Visual mood:** solitude, patience, exposure. **Documentary realism:** real-speed motion, plausible water movement against the hull, weathered paint, wet rope; desaturated natural colour, no dramatic sky, no cinematic haze beams. **Aspect ratio:** 16:9, 3840 × 2160, 25 fps, 4 seconds.

*Accuracy check:* confirm the dory's shape, colour and gear against boats in the Ílhavo museum before generating, and revise the prompt to match. No outboard motor, no life jacket, no modern materials, no mother ship unless the museum supplies a reference. The on-screen caption *Reconstruction* stays for the full shot.
